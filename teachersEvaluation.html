Teachers Evaluation Form (by Student)
Instructions: This questionnaire is given to you for your opinion about any teacher of your phase. Your opinion is very valuable for the improvement of the academic activities. Your identity will not be disclosed by any way because the questionnaire is anonymous. So your full co-operation is expected.
The number rating stands for the following: 1 = Never, 2 = Once in a while, 3 = Sometimes, 4 = Most of the time, 5 = Almost always. If not applicable, leave it blank.
Circle/select the number that best represents your experience with this teacher for each item. Please consider each item carefully and respond honestly.
